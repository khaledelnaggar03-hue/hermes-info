Privacy policy

Hermes is a private tool with one user: its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Google Sheets data, with the owner's consent.
  • That data is used only to prepare reminders and drafts for the owner.
  • Data is stored on the owner's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
